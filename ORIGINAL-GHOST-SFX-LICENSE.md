# Ghost celebration audio — source and licensing

Halloween Theme's 1.40.8 ghost sound pool contains three **different**
OpenGameArt recordings. See the bundled
`FileCopies/GHOST_AUDIO_CREDITS.txt` for creator credits, direct source URLs,
applicable licenses, and the modifications used to convert the files into OGG.

- "Ghost" — Ogrebane — CC0 1.0
- "Scary ghost 1" — The Berklee College of Music (submitted by qubodup) — CC BY 3.0
- "Ghost breath" — qubodup — CC0 1.0

**CC BY 3.0 requires attribution.** Retain the credits file in the QMOD and
source distribution, or reproduce its relevant details in release notes.
The original three sounds are converted to OGG Vorbis and adjusted to 80%
level; no other pitch or tempo changes.

The optional Uppbeat Halloween Music Sting is separate and not cleared for
public QMOD redistribution. Credits here do not license unrelated menu audio.
