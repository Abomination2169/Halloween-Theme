These sound effects are incorporated into the complete, free, functioning Beat Saber
QMOD. **They are not distributed as a separate sound-effect collection.**

republication of the source sound files; the two Pixabay clips are supplied as
components of this creative mod. A request for specific clarification of QMOD
distribution was sent to Pixabay; no additional individual permission is claimed.

Detailed credit records are also installed as `GHOST_AUDIO_CREDITS.txt` and
`PIXABAY_AUDIO_CREDITS.txt`. The separately sourced Uppbeat musical sting
is not part of this public release.
